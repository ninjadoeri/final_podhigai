A Midday Meal You Choose

There is no fixed lunch menu here. Our kitchen builds the afternoon meal around what your family or group actually wants to eat.

  • Full rice meals, or lighter plates if you prefer
  • Pick the gravy, the poriyals and the sides
  • Vegetarian, non-vegetarian and Jain kept apart in the kitchen
  • Festive thalis for birthdays and family get-togethers

Share your choices over morning coffee and lunch will be waiting.

Dinner, Your Way

Dinner is cooked to order. Tell us what your group feels like eating and our kitchen prepares it fresh that evening.

  • Veg, non-veg or Jain — cooked separately
  • Biryani, chapati, dosa or rice meals on request
  • Spice level made mild or strong as you prefer
  • Late dinner and celebration menus can be arranged

Place your request by evening and we will have it ready for you.
